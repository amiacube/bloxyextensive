## 🛠️What does it change?

- Adds items and mobs from other gamemodes to survival
- Increases game's difficulty 
- Adds 2 bossfights
- Adds its own progression
- Adds QoL features

## 🎮How to play it?

Create a lobby in Sandbox survival with these settings:
- Enable Day & Night — OFF
- Survival Guns — OFF

When you enter the world, press the F8 key to open the World Code, and replace its entire contents with the code from the mod.js file from this repository.

## 🏹Progression Guide

At the start of the game, you should explore the caves to obtain basic gear and, most importantly, a moonstone. 

Once you have the moonstone, you can craft a Moon Chest. Place 1 moonstone and 30 units of any food with a special effect (such as plums, which increase damage) inside it to make that effect permanent. 

After obtaining a few buffs, you should craft a glider to find the snow biome, where you'll need to obtain the Knight Heart from the Draugr Knights.

Using 5 Rotten Brains, 10 Rotten Flesh, and 1 Knight's Heart, you can craft a Crone Spawn Orb, an item that summons the first boss. The best weapon for defeating the boss is an enchanted diamond crossbow. Armor doesn't matter, because the boss will kill you with a single hit anyway.

After killing the boss, you'll get a few guns, unlock several crafting recipes, and Stalkers will start spawning in the world.

To use a guns, you'll need ammunition, which you can craft using 1 gold and 2 iron bars. You can also farm boss for better gun, the best possible drop is the Minigun.

After that, you'll need to farm Stalkers to obtain the Draugr Reaver Spawn Orb, the summoning item for the final boss.

The boss fight is very different from the first one. After defeating the Reaver, you’ll provoke the god’s wrath, and the real boss fight will begin. You’ll have to survive 60 seconds of rapid attacks from Warpers and Magma Golems.

If you managed to pass the final challenge, congratulations — you've completed the mod!

## ⭐All features list
**General:**
- After respawning, you will see message with your death location and deaths counter.
- KeepInvetory enabled.
- Sky's color changes to simulate day-night cycle.
- By using any emote, you will activate aura boost. You will lose 1 aura level per second but get x2 damage.

**Mobs:**
- Changed mobs stats.
- After killing a mob, Warper will spawn.
- After killing Cave/Frost Golem, Magma Golem will spawn.
- After getting hit by a mob, aura will drop to 0 and some of the mob's stats will increase.
- Slimes are now spawning without Day & Night enabled.
- Crone reworked to be a boss.
- 1/3 chance to spawn Stalker, instead of any mob, after killing Crone.

**Crafts:**
- Added crafts for all Spears, Daggers, Clubs, Maces, Whips and Boomerangs.
- Gliders are now require 1 moonstone to craft.
- Added craft for Ammo.
- Added crafts for all vehicles.
- Added crafts for all Jump Pads and Landing Pad.
- Added craft for Crone Spawn Orb.
- Added craft for Kill Spikes.
- Added crafts for Frag Grenade, Smoke Grenade and Super RPG, if Crone was killed.
- Added craft for Crystal.
- Added craft for Moonstone Remote Explosive.
