# 🔎 Deep Research Thinking Feed
> **The autonomous investigation system for complex Bloxd.io research, verification, and executive-grade synthesis.**

**Deep Research** is a specialized reasoning and investigation engine designed to break down complicated prompts into structured analytical workflows. Instead of responding with a single-shot answer, it builds a dynamic research plan, gathers evidence, verifies findings, and produces a polished report grounded in live sources and structured analysis.

***

## 🚀 Core Philosophy

Many AI systems can answer fast, but few can truly investigate. Deep Research is designed to behave more like an analytical team than a simple chatbot. It does not just retrieve information—it decomposes a problem, maps the missing pieces, and pursues them through iterative research loops until the answer is well-supported.

This approach is especially important for complex Bloxd.io topics, where accuracy, source quality, and technical specificity matter. Deep Research is built to reduce guesswork, surface contradictions, and improve confidence before finalizing an answer.

***

## ✨ Key Features & Capabilities

### 🧠 Intent Decomposition
The system begins by dissecting a prompt into its primary objectives and hidden sub-questions. This allows it to detect what must be answered, what must be verified, and what likely requires deeper investigation.

### 🔬 Multi-Step Investigation Engine
Deep Research does not stop at the first few search results. It builds a layered research workflow that can:
*   **Formulate initial search queries** based on the user’s intent.
*   **Identify relevant domains** such as official documentation, community resources, or technical references.
*   **Launch recursive follow-up queries** when a gap or contradiction appears.
*   **Track confidence thresholds** before finalizing findings.

### 📚 Source Evaluation & Verification
As information is collected, the system evaluates:
*   **Information density**
*   **Relevance to the original goal**
*   **Evidence strength**
*   **Need for deeper follow-up research**

This helps avoid unsupported claims and improves report quality.

### 🧾 Executive-Grade Report Synthesis
Once the investigation converges, Deep Research organizes its findings into a polished Markdown report featuring:
*   Inline citations
*   Structural summaries
*   Key takeaways
*   Verification-aware conclusions

***

## 🛠️ System Workflow: From Prompt to Report

1.  **Intent Intake:** The user submits a complex question or research request.
2.  **Plan Formation:** The system identifies the main objectives and hidden sub-questions.
3.  **Execution Graph Construction:** It builds a dynamic graph of research steps, search targets, and validation criteria.
4.  **Live Investigation:** The engine retrieves and evaluates relevant sources in real time.
5.  **Gap Detection & Recursive Follow-Up:** If a contradiction or missing detail is found, it launches deeper queries automatically.
6.  **Report Synthesis:** Once confidence thresholds are met, the system compiles the findings into a comprehensive report.

***

## 🧠 Thinking Feed Experience

The Thinking Feed is the live, interactive surface that reveals how Deep Research is operating in real time. Instead of showing a static loading animation, it exposes the reasoning process in a transparent and structured way.

### 🔄 UI Structure
The Thinking Feed is designed as a collapsible, interactive accordion experience featuring:
*   **Pulsing status badges** for active research stages.
*   **Animated progress indicators** for each step.
*   **Live source pill badges** showing active sub-queries.
*   **Expandable detail panels** that expose the decision-making behind each step.

This creates a sense of transparency while preserving a high-level overview of the investigation process.

***

## 📌 Why This Matters

Deep Research is not simply about retrieving more data. It is about organizing reasoning in a way that is rigorous, visible, and useful. The Thinking Feed makes the investigation process legible so users can see:
*   What the system is trying to solve.
*   What it is currently examining.
*   When it is seeking more evidence.
*   How it arrives at its final conclusions.

That transparency makes the feature more trustworthy, especially for technical or competitive research topics.
