SECTION 1 — WHAT IS BLOXD.IO (OVERVIEW & FULL EXPERIENCE)
---------------------------------------------------------------------

Bloxd.io is a free, browser-based multiplayer sandbox game built around blocky, voxel-style worlds. Players join servers and lobbies to play a wide variety of game modes, build and mine in shared worlds, fight other players, and create their own custom games using an in-game JavaScript-based scripting system (World Code and Code Blocks). It runs directly in the browser and is playable on both PC and mobile, though PvP is generally easier and more precise on PC. At its core, much of Bloxd.io is built around PvP (player-versus-player combat), but the game spans far beyond fighting — it includes survival, building, parkour, minigames, roleplay-style modes, and fully player-scripted experiences.

The game is organized into GAME MODES. New modes are frequently introduced through a "gamemode voting" system, where two modes are released at once and the community effectively votes with playtime — whichever is played more stays, and the other is often moved to a "Legacy Games" category or removed. Because of this, the mode lineup changes over time. Existing and popular modes include Survival, Worlds, Bedwars, Skywars, One Block, Greenville, Creative, Pirates, Lucky Towers, Cube Warfare, Paintball, Infection, Rocket Spleef, Fishing, 1v1 Fights, Lucky Block Battlegrounds, Natural Disaster, Bridge Battle, Hide and Seek, 99 Nights, Frontline, Containment Breach, Core Defenders, The Bloxd SMP, Murder Mystery, Sky Wars, Plots, Sky Islands, and many more. Limited-time modes (added without voting) have included The Bloxd SMP, Survival with Guns, Day & Night Survival, Build a Factory, and Chunklock.

The main pillars of the Bloxd.io experience are:

BUILDING & CREATIVITY — Players place and break blocks to build structures, bases, cities, dungeons, parkour courses, and adventure maps. There is a huge palette of blocks (over a thousand block types), including natural blocks, colored variants (wool, concrete, glass, planks, ceramics, chalk), decorative blocks, and functional blocks. Creative mode and Worlds let players build freely and design their own spaces.

SURVIVAL & PROGRESSION — In survival-style modes, players gather resources (wood, stone, ores like coal, iron, gold, diamond, moonstone, and lunite), mine, craft tools and gear at a Workbench or Artisan Bench, farm crops and food, tame/catch mobs and pets, and progress from basic wood gear up to diamond, moonstone, and specialized equipment. Players also manage hunger/health and can build automated systems (Auto-Miner, Auto-Crafter, Auto-Harvester).

COMBAT (PvP) — Combat is spam-click based (similar to Minecraft 1.8/Bedrock PvP): you click to attack, but hits only register once every 0.4 seconds (about 2.5 hits/second max), so the ideal effective CPS is around 12–16 (up to 20 with control), and well-timed hits matter more than raw click speed. Critical hits (hitting an enemy while you are in midair) deal 1.3x (30% more) damage. The first hit while sprinting deals extra knockback (used in "sprint-cut" combos). Combat uses swords, axes, bows, crossbows, spears, daggers, clubs, maces, whips, boomerangs, potions, splash potions, effect arrows, fireballs, iceballs, moonstone explosives, moonstone orbs (throwable teleport, deals 15 self-damage per throw), guns and RPGs (only obtainable via interactive boards or code blocks in worlds), plus improvised weapons like sticks (knockback), pebbles, snowballs, fat cactus, mangoes, and coconuts. IMPORTANT: cheating (autoclicking, drag-clicking, or exceeding ~20 CPS) can get a player auto-banned because the game flags it as an autoclicker.

PROTECTION & MULTIPLAYER — Since worlds are shared, players use Protector blocks (and Tribe/Faction Protectors) to claim land and prevent griefing (block breaking, placing, chest access) within a protected region.

CUSTOM WORLDS & SCRIPTING — This is a defining feature: players who own worlds can script custom gameplay using Bloxd's JavaScript-based coding system. World Code (opened with F8) runs globally in response to game events via callbacks (onPlayerJoin, tick, etc.), and Code Blocks are placed in the world and run when clicked/interacted with. Through the api object, scripts can control players, blocks, mobs, inventories, messages, effects, UI, and persistent data — enabling fully custom game modes, minigames, teleporters, shops, boss fights, and more. (Detailed API/scripting knowledge is in the scripting section.)

COMMUNITY — Bloxd.io has official in-game community rules (respect others; no harassment/bullying, hate speech, spam, links, NSFW content, online dating, impersonation, cheating/autoclicking, or userscripts/extensions/mods). NOTE: Bloxd.io (the game) rules are SEPARATE from BloxdHub (the community platform) rules — never mix them.


HISTORY & ORIGIN
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Bloxd.io was created by Arthur Baker (username "Arthur"), a UK developer born November 1999 and the founder/owner of the game. He built Bloxd.io using a free open-source voxel game engine called Noa. Before Bloxd.io, Arthur worked at Veea Systems (software dev intern), taught first-year algorithms at the University of Bath, interned at Microsoft as a Product Manager, and worked at Improbable as a software engineer (where he built tech for massive multiplayer worlds in Unreal Engine). Microsoft's acquisition of Minecraft was part of what drove him to create Bloxd.io. He has been developing the game for roughly 5 years. Arthur personally created about 15 of the game modes, including Evil Tower, Doodle Cube, Cube Warfare, Peaceful, Bloxd (Gamemode), Survival, Creative, Bedwars, Worlds, Duels, and Hide and Seek; his first mode, Bloxd Hop, was made with his own engine alongside a collaborator (Weiqing). The game was originally known under earlier names before becoming "Bloxd.io."

CORE PLAYER MECHANICS
---------------------------------------------------------------------
HEALTH & COMBAT: Players have a health bar; combat is click-based with hits registering once every 0.4 seconds (max ~2.5 landed hits/sec), ideal effective CPS ~12-16. Critical hits (attacking while airborne) deal 1.3x damage. Armour reduces incoming damage but splash poison and instant-damage potions bypass armour. Health regenerates over time and can be restored with food and potions.

AURA XP (THE EXPERIENCE / LEVELING SYSTEM): Aura XP is Bloxd's experience system, shown as a bar above the hotbar with a star icon showing your current level. Key mechanics:
- You LOSE exactly HALF your XP levels when you die.
- When you KILL someone, you gain the amount they lost (half their XP); if they had none, you get 1 level.
- Maximum is 300 levels (can be changed via code blocks).
- Earned from: killing mobs (Cow/Pig/Sheep/Deer = 20, Horse = 25, Wolf/Wildcat/Stag/Draugr Skeleton = 30, Gorilla/Bear/Draugr Zombie = 40, Cave Golem = 50, Draugr Knight = 75), mining ores (Coal = 5, Iron = 15, Gold = 30, Diamond = 40, Moonstone = 50), smelting (Iron = 10, Gold = 15), harvesting crops, breaking mob spawners, and consuming Aura XP items (Fragment = 1 level, Orb = 3 levels, Potion = 10 levels, Potion II = 15 levels; splash versions are instant).
- Aura XP is SPENT on enchanting armour and tools.

ENCHANTING: Done at Enchantment Tables (5 tiers: wooden, stone, iron, gold, diamond — higher tiers give better odds of stronger enchants). Enchantment effects come in tiers I, II, III (increasing power); items are enchanted at tiers 1-5 (the number = total combined enchant levels, e.g. tier 5 could give a III + a II). Enchanting spends Aura XP levels, and results are RANDOM — you pick from three XP "prices" (higher price = better odds), but can't choose exact enchants. Re-enchanting overwrites previous enchantments.
Enchantments by item type:
- ALL ARMOR: Health (+4 max HP per level), Health Regen, Protection (reduces damage). Fur Chestplate also: Knockback Resist. Spiked Boots also: Stomp Damage.
- SWORDS: Damage (+20%/32%/40% by level), Attack Speed, Critical Damage (+42%/51%/58% by level). Knight Sword also: Horizontal Knockback.
- STICK: Damage, Horizontal Knockback, Vertical Knockback.
- ALL TOOLS: Break Speed, Momentum (faster breaking as you break more).
- PICKAXE (except Moonstone): Mining Aura (more XP mining), Mining Yield (more drops).
- SPADE: Digging Aura. HOE: Farming Aura, Farming Yield. AXE: Lumber Aura.
- BOW/CROSSBOW: Arrow Speed, Arrow Damage (+23.5%/35%/44% by level), Quick Charge.

CRAFTING: The Workbench is the core crafting station (hand-craftable from 4 Wood Planks of any type). It crafts tools, armour, weapons, utilities, and equipment. Decorative blocks require the Artisan Bench. Other stations: Furnace (smelting ores — 8 stone to craft), Potion Table (2 Glass + 2 Wood Planks), Salvaging Table (2 Iron Bars + 2 Wood Planks; salvages minerals into Aura XP Fragments), Enchantment Tables. You can only craft when you have all materials AND an empty inventory slot for the output. Armour costs: Helmet = 5, Chestplate = 8, Leggings = 7, Boots = 4, Gauntlets = 4 of the material (+ full set = 26 total).

MINING & ORE DEPTH: Ores are found underground at depth (y-levels): iron roughly y -15 to -80, gold roughly y -75 to -99, diamond roughly y -85 to -99, moonstone y -84 and below. Better pickaxes mine higher-tier ores (iron pick for gold, gold pick for diamond, diamond pick for moonstone/obsidian). Moonstone pickaxe drops the exact block and mines fastest.

PROGRESSION LOOP (survival-style modes): gather wood → craft basic tools → mine stone → better tools → mine deeper ores (iron→gold→diamond→moonstone) → craft armour/weapons → farm Aura XP → enchant gear → build/protect a base → PvP or take on tougher mobs (Draugr, golems, bosses).

MOVEMENT & UTILITY: Hang Gliders (Wood/Iron/Gold/Diamond) for gliding and fall protection; Boats/Speedboats/Obsidian Boats for water; Moonstone Orbs for throwable teleportation; jump pads and spears for mobility; double-jump available via client options in scripted worlds.

GENERAL / META
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- PLATFORM: free, browser-based, PC + mobile. PC PvP is more precise than mobile.
- GAMEMODE VOTING: new modes are added in pairs and the community "votes" by playtime; losers go to Legacy Games or are removed. Mode lineup rotates frequently.
- ANTI-CHEAT: autoclicking, drag-clicking, or exceeding ~20 CPS can trigger an automatic ban (flagged as autoclicker). Cheating, userscripts, extensions, and mods are against the rules.
- DATA PERSISTENCE: worlds and player progress can be saved; scripted worlds use player DB values (per-account) and lobby DB values (world-wide).

SECTION 3 — ALL BLOCKS
=====================================================================
NOTE: Blocks make up the entire world and all gamemode maps. They sit in a 3D grid
of 1m cubic cells. Some are solid/opaque (dirt, stone), others transparent/non-solid
(glass, flowers, leaves). Explosions destroy some blocks easily and some are immune.
Items/tools/armor/weapons are covered in SECTION 2 and are NOT repeated here.
Verified from the official Bloxd.io Fandom wiki (Blocks, Natural Blocks, Stone Variants,
Ores, Natural Soils pages), July 2026.

Best mining tool legend: Spade = soft ground, Pickaxe = stone/ore, Axe = wood,
Shears/Hoe = leaves/grass/plants. "Moonstone Pickaxe" drops the ORE/STONE block
itself instead of the messy/mineral drop.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
NATURAL SOIL & GROUND BLOCKS
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- Grass Block (Spade) — surface of plains/forests. Shear it → turns to Dirt + drops a
  Grass Block (renewable infinite grass/dirt). Hoe it → Tilled Soil.
- Dirt (Spade) — 4 layers under any grass before stone. Craft: 2 Dirt → 1 Messy Dirt;
  2 Dirt → 4 Dirt Slabs; Messy Stone + Dirt → Rocky Dirt.
- Rocky Dirt (Spade) — natural in mountains/hills, or craft 1 Messy Stone + 1 Dirt.
- Messy Dirt (crafted from 2 Dirt; reversible to 2 Dirt).
- Sand (Spade) — deserts, riverbeds. Smelt → Glass; used for Sandstone, Bricks, bottles.
- Red Sand (Spade) — badlands surface. Boosts cactus growth (+1 max height) & plant growth (+30%).
- Snow (Spade) — snowy plains, mountain tops. Craft Snow + Ice → Ice Bricks.
- Packed Snow (Spade) — frozen badlands. Crafted from 2 Snow.
- Clay (Spade) — riverbeds. → Baked Clay (dyeable), Concrete, Bricks, decorative blocks.
- Chalk (Spade/Pickaxe) — mined from rivers; dyeable at Artisan Bench.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
STONE & STONE VARIANTS (Pickaxe)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- Stone — always 5 blocks under surface, and exposed on steep hills. Drops MESSY STONE
  when mined with any pickaxe EXCEPT the Moonstone Pickaxe (which drops Stone itself).
  Smelt 4 Messy Stone + 1 Coal in a Furnace → Stone.
- Andesite (grayish-blue) — Andesite Pit biome. Drops itself. → Smooth/Engraved/Bricks.
- Granite (brown) — Granite Valley biome. Drops itself. → Smooth/Engraved/Bricks.
- Diorite (white w/ gray squares) — Diorite Cave biome. Drops itself. → Smooth/Engraved/Bricks.
- Sandstone & Red Sandstone — crafted from Sand / Red Sand (NOT true stone; can't sub
  for stone in recipes). Each → Smooth, Marked, Engraved versions.

MESSY STONE COMPRESSION CHAIN (each = 9 of the previous, decorative/storage):
Messy Stone → Compressed → Extra Compressed → Super Compressed → Hyper Compressed →
Ultra Compressed → Mega Compressed Messy Stone.
Other Messy Stone variants: Mossy Messy Stone (Messy Stone + grass/tall grass/cactus),
Snowy Messy Stone (Messy Stone + snow), Carved Messy Stone (2 Messy Stone).

Stone-family block breaking time (Stone, seconds): Fists 4.65 (no drop) | Wood Pick 1.00 |
Stone Pick 0.66 | Iron Pick 0.40 | Gold Pick 0.34 | Golem Pick 0.28 | Diamond Pick 0.21.

Ice (Pickaxe) — forms on top of water in snowy biomes. +1.4 walk speed on it. → Iceballs, Ice Bricks/Slabs.

ORES (see SECTION 1B for full mining/XP detail; block facts here)

BIOMES (21 total)

Biomes are distinct world areas; each offers different blocks/resources.

GRASS-BASED (grass block surface; common: trees, grass, tall grass, flowers; rare:
melons, watermelons, pumpkins, cotton, rice, forest mushrooms, corn. Structures:
Mysterious Ruins (rare), Rivers (common), Lava Pools (uncommon), Great Maple Trees):
- Plains — sparse maple trees; all grass-based resources except mushrooms.
- Grassy Plains — plains variant, heavy grass/tall grass cover.
- Meadows — plains variant, flower-covered; ONLY biome with Corn; extra Cotton.
- Maple Forests — dense maple + occasional aspen/plum/pear (only one of the three);
  no cotton/rice in forests, but fat mushrooms spawn.
- Pear / Aspen / Plum Forests — forest of that tree type plus some maple.
- Bluebell Forests — rare maple-forest variant; ONLY source of Bluebells (cover ground).
- Autumn Forests — maple-forest variant; orange/yellow autumn leaves, fallen leaves, autumn ferns.
- Cherry Blossom Forests — cherry trees + occasional maple/aspen; fallen cherry leaves; NO structures.

SAND-BASED (flat, sand surface; resources: cactus, fat cactus, chili peppers (rare).
Structures: Lava Pools, Rivers):
- Deserts — standard sand-based biome.
- Cactus Fields — rare desert variant, huge amount of cacti/fat cacti.
- Red Deserts — rare desert variant with Red Sand surface.

SNOW-BASED (trees + snow on ground; rivers get an Ice top layer):
- Snowy Plains — plains-like, cranberry patches, snow ground, no grass, maple trees still spawn.
- Snowy Pine / Cedar Forests — pine/cedar forest variants with snow surface.
- Frozen Badlands — MOST DANGEROUS biome (also most rewarding). Packed Snow surface,
  Snow instead of dirt. Structures: Graves, Skull Temples, large Ice Spikes (often
  containing Diamond/Moonstone ore). Frozen undead mobs: Draugr Zombies, Frost Golems,
  Frost Skeletons, Draugr Knights, + Spectral Wolf. Spectral Trees / Great Spectral Trees.

OTHER FOREST BIOMES (unique, don't share traits):
- Spectral Forest — packed with Spectral Trees (sometimes Great); occasional ruins.
- Pine Forest — mostly Pine Trees, occasional Cedar.
- Cedar Forest — mostly Cedar Trees.
- Jungle — jungle trees.

CAVE BIOMES (underground; see SECTION 3 for ores):
- Stony Caves, Andesite Caves, Granite Caves (Granite Valley), Diorite Caves.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
STRUCTURES
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Structures = groups of blocks placed in an unnatural way (stone bricks, etc.), generated
naturally in Open World gamemodes (Survival, Peaceful, Creative, Factions). Trees and
caves are NOT structures. 5 core types + terrain features. Loot chest tiers:
common → uncommon → rare → epic → legendary (higher = better loot).

1. MYSTERIOUS RUINS (surface) — Plains & Forests (NOT Cherry Blossom). ~20 variations.
   Built from Messy Stone / Compressed Messy Stone / Stone Brick variants; almost all
   have Vines; bigger ones add Wood + Patterned Glass. Forms: castles, churches, walls,
   statues, forts, colosseums. Loot: Food, smelted Ores, Tools/Armor, building materials
   (Stone/Concrete/Ceramics) — from coal/bread up to tier-5 enchanted diamond gear,
   bouncy bombs, spirit animals by chest tier. NO Diamonds/Moonstone/Diamond gear EXCEPT
   secret chests. Some chests buried (bring a Spade). Spawners always hostile (Cave Golem,
   Draugr Zombie, or Draugr Skeleton). Each ruin type has 1 hidden best-loot chest.

2. LAVA POOLS (anywhere, above/underground; can be a straight "lavafall") — medium lava
   pool; surrounding blocks replaced with Magma, Compressed Messy Stone, Stone. If there's
   no magma/stone border or it's arranged neatly, it's likely player-made, not natural.

3. JUNGLE RUINS (Jungle biome) — 3 variants: 1 chalk-brick type (chalk bricks, brown
   mushrooms, stone spikes, cobwebs, dangling vine, diamond/coal ore, jungle blocks,
   loot chest) + 2 waterfall types (straight w/ center loot chamber; corner w/ chamber chest).

4. FROZEN RUINS (Frozen Badlands — best looting biome, 5–10 per biome) — 6 variations.
   Built from Stone Slabs, Messy Stone, Spruce/Pine Planks, Spectral Log, Dirt, Spawners
   (all hostile). Variants: gigantic Skull Temples, small graveyards, 2 hut types (spike-
   trap hut w/ graveyard + 1 spawner; larger hut w/ 3 chests + 1 spawner); some spawn like
   normal ruins. SKULL TEMPLE chests (normal AND secret): can contain Diamonds, Moonstone,
   Diamond gear (Moonstone gear sometimes) — the high-value frozen loot.

5. UNDERGROUND RUINS (caves; added Oct 2025) — walls always include Messy Stone (easy to
   spot when near, though rare). Introduced Magma Golem & Draugr Huntress to Survival. Variants:
   - MAGMA GOLEM RUIN — largest; hollowed cave w/ stone pillars, a ring/"donut" of Magma +
     Lava w/ a center magma island. 4 Magma Golem Spawners. 4 chests (2 uncommon/rare around
     the donut; 2 GUARANTEED EPIC, chance LEGENDARY on the island). Diamond & Moonstone Ore
     at the bottom of the lava. Also spawns Draugr Zombies/Skeletons, Cave Golems (+ under a
     frozen biome: Frost Golems/Zombies/Skeletons, Draugr Knights). Epic/legendary loot:
     gold, diamonds, moonstone, epic/legendary-enchanted Fur Chestplates/Spiked Boots/gold &
     diamond armor, Aura XP Potions I/II, Chaos Potions (rare), spirit animals (Gorilla/Bear/
     Stag/Wolf/Golem — guaranteed in legendary chest), firecrackers, arrows, blocks, food.
   - LARGE ARENAS — biggest arena; 2 spawners (Draugr Huntress/Skeleton/Zombie/Cave Golem),
     smooth stone pillars, sunken middle. Draugr Banners spawn here. 2 chests epic/legendary.
   - SMALL ARENAS — small sunken platform; 1 Draugr Huntress OR Cave Golem spawner + 2 chests.
     Draugr Banners on walls.
   - DUNGEONS (Large Dungeon) — Stone/Smooth Stone/Mossy Messy Stone/Andesite/Stone Bricks/
     Cracked & Mossy Bricks + chests. Multiple spawner types. ONLY place to find the Draugr
     Banner. 2 chests epic/legendary. One variant resembles Minecraft Trail Ruins.
   - Also: Small Dungeon + standalone Spawner blocks generate underground/in caves.

TERRAIN FEATURES (natural formations, not "structures"):
- Rivers — common in grass/sand/snow biomes; snowy-biome rivers get an Ice top layer.
- Ice Spikes — large spikes across Frozen Badlands; many contain ore (incl. Diamond/Moonstone).
- Great Trees — uncommon oversized trees: Great Maple (grass biomes), Great Spectral
  (Spectral Forest / Frozen Badlands).
- Graves — small grave formations in Frozen Badlands (part of the Frozen Ruins family).

FACTIONS PRESET STRUCTURES (utility, not random world-gen): Shop, Camps, Sand Mine, Wood Mill
(like Greenville's work stations).

=====================================================================
SECTION 6 — CODING (BLOXD WORLD CODE, CODE BLOCKS & THE api.* OBJECT)
=====================================================================
This is the section for writing Bloxd code (the "Orbit" domain). Verified from the
official Bloxd.io docs (bloxd.io/docs), the Bloxd Fandom "Code Block"/"JavaScript API
Functions" pages, and the official code-api GitHub. July 2026.
CRITICAL: Every function lives on the `api.` object. NEVER invent functions like
`inventory.addItem` or `getPlayerPosition` — they do not exist. Only use verified names.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
THE THREE WAYS TO RUN CODE
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- WORLD CODE — main game logic. Press F8 (world owners only). Runs on CALLBACK EVENTS
  (onPlayerJoin, tick, etc.). Supports ALL callbacks. 16,000 char limit. myId = null,
  thisPos = null (no clicker/position context).
- CODE BLOCKS — physical blocks from the creative menu. Right-click to edit. Run when
  clicked or triggered by an adjacent "Press to Code" board. CANNOT use callbacks.
  16,000 chars each. myId = the player who clicked; thisPos = the block's [x,y,z].
- CODE BOARDS — a sign whose text starts with "press to code"; runs JS on right-click.
  Tiny space (~96 chars / 16×6). Use Code Blocks for anything longer.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
THE CODE ENVIRONMENT (QuickJS) — HARD CONSTRAINTS
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Bloxd runs code in QuickJS, a limited JS engine. This means:
- NO async/await — QuickJS is synchronous only. Cannot use async functions.
- NO setTimeout, setInterval, Promises, window, document, fetch, or browser APIs.
- For timing/delays: use the `tick` callback + `api.now()` (ms timestamp) to track
  elapsed time. Store per-player state in GLOBAL variables (declared with `var`, or
  assigned without a keyword) so it persists across ticks.
- AVOID `let`/`const`/`var` in GLOBAL scope (outside a function) — it can break the script.
  Either wrap code in a function `(() => { ... })()`, or assign globals WITHOUT a keyword.
- Code runs in ISOLATION per Code Block and RESETS each run — no saved state unless you
  use a global (`var` globals are shared across World Code, Code Blocks & Boards).
- Comments work: `// line` and `/* block */`. Debug with `api.log("x")` or `console.log("x")`.
- Standard JS (Math, arrays, objects, for/while, destructuring, arrow funcs) all work.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
GLOBAL VARIABLES (auto-available)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- myId / playerId — player ID of whoever triggered the code (null in World Code). Also
  works as entityId (players AND mobs share the entityId concept).
- thisPos — [x, y, z] of the Code Block/Board running (null in World Code).
- api — the global object holding ALL API methods.

CALLBACKS (World Code only — 64 total; assign or define, DON'T prefix with api.)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Callbacks are functions YOU define/assign; the engine calls them. Two valid forms:
    function onPlayerJoin(id) { ... }
    onPlayerJoin = (id) => { ... }
NEVER call a callback yourself and NEVER write api.onPlayerJoin. World Code re-runs when
it's updated or when the lobby comes back online.

Full callback list (64):
tick, onClose, onPlayerJoin, onPlayerLeave, onPlayerJump, onRespawnRequest, playerCommand,
onPlayerChat, onPlayerChangeBlock, onPlayerDropItem, onPlayerPickedUpItem,

onPlayerMoveInvenItemWithAmt, onPlayerAttemptAltAction, onPlayerAltAction, onPlayerClick,

onPlayerDamagingMob, onMobDamagingPlayer, onMobDamagingOtherMob, onAttemptKillPlayer,

KEY CALLBACK SIGNATURES (verified):
- tick — NO parameters. Runs repeatedly. Do timing here with api.now(); iterate players
  via api.getPlayerIds(). Do NOT assume tick = 1/second or that playerId exists in it.
- onPlayerJoin(id) — one param: the joining player's id. (A second param exists on some
  versions; official docs use fromGameReset, NOT "fromSameServer".)
- onBlockStand(id, x, y, z, blockName) — fires when a player stands on a block.
- onPlayerDamagingOtherPlayer(damagedId, pid, damage, weaponName) — pid = attacker.
- onPlayerDamagingMob(damagedId, pid, damage, weaponName)
- onPlayerKilledOtherPlayer(a, b, c, d) — a = killer id (use to reward the killer).

---------------------------------------------------------------------
VERIFIED WORKING EXAMPLES (safe patterns to imitate)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
// Welcome message on join
function onPlayerJoin(id) {
  api.sendTopRightHelper(id, "star", "WELCOME!", {color:"yellow", duration:9})
}

// Trampoline block
onBlockStand = (id, x, y, z, blockName) => {
  if (blockName === "Red Chalk") { api.setVelocity(id, 0, 20, 0) }
}

// Freeze + damage on hit with a Diamond Sword
onPlayerDamagingOtherPlayer = (damagedId, pid, d, w) => {
  if (w === "Diamond Sword") {
    api.applyHealthChange(pid, -30)
    api.applyEffect(pid, "Frozen", 5000, {inbuiltLevel:1})
  }
}

// Drop a coin when a player kills another (item name is SINGULAR)
onPlayerKilledOtherPlayer = (a, b, c, d) => api.giveItem(a, "Gold Coin", 1)

// Jump pad (Code Block)
api.setVelocity(myId, 0, 12, 0)

// Spawn an "Iron Golem" (reskinned Cave Golem)
let [x, y, z] = thisPos
api.attemptSpawnMob("Cave Golem", x, y, z, {name:"Iron Golem", variation:"iron"})

// Timing WITHOUT setTimeout — global state + tick + api.now()
var lastFire = {}
tick = () => {
  let t = api.now()
  for (const id of api.getPlayerIds()) {
    if (!lastFire[id] || t - lastFire[id] > 5000) {
      lastFire[id] = t
      // ...do the every-5-seconds thing for this player...
    }
  }
}

SECTION 7 — CRAFTING RECIPES (FURNACE, WORKBENCH, ARTISAN BENCH)
=====================================================================
Bloxd has three crafting stations plus handcrafting (some items craft directly from the
inventory with no station). Verified from the official Bloxd.io Fandom wiki (Workbench,
Furnace/Smeltable Items, Artisan Bench, Gold pages), July 2026.
NOTE: "Wood Planks" = planks of ANY tree type unless specified. "Stone" in recipes can
usually be substituted with Messy Stone / Andesite / Granite / Diorite. Wiki flags its
recipe tables as incomplete, so this is the largest verified set, not 100% exhaustive.

STATION ROLES:
- FURNACE (8 Messy Stone) — smelts ores/raw materials/food (needs Coal as fuel).
- WORKBENCH (4 Wood Planks) — tools, weapons, armor, utilities, most survival gear.
- ARTISAN BENCH (2 Wood Planks + 2 Stone) — decorative blocks, colored items, compression
  blocks, banners/carpets. Also handcrafts anything handcraftable.
- HANDCRAFT (no station) — Workbench, Wood Planks, and a few basics craft from inventory.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
FURNACE — SMELTING (each smelt needs Coal as fuel)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- 1 Raw Iron + 1 Coal → 1 Iron Bar   (also: 1 Iron Ore + 1 Coal → 1 Iron Bar)
- 1 Raw Gold + 1 Coal → 1 Gold Bar   (also: 1 Gold Ore + 1 Coal → 1 Gold Bar)
- Diamond Ore + Coal → Diamonds (4 Diamond Ore → 4 Diamonds)
- Moonstone Ore + Coal → Moonstone

- Sand → Glass (smelt sand; used for bottles, potion tables, windows)
- Magma → (smeltable material)
- Coal Ore → Coal
- Barkless Logs, Wheat → (processed forms)
- FOOD (raw → cooked): Raw Porkchop → Cooked Porkchop; Raw Beef → Steak; Raw Mutton →
  Cooked Mutton; Raw Venison → Cooked Venison; Potato → (baked).
- Rotten Flesh, Rotten Brain → smeltable (processing).
- Dark Red Stone → (smeltable variant).

AURA XP — FULL VALUE TABLE (SEC 1B has the mechanics; here are the numbers)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Bar sits above the hotbar. On death: lose HALF your XP levels. Kill a player: gain what
they lost (half theirs; min 1 if they had none). Max 300 levels (raisable via Code Blocks).
Spent on enchanting (SEC 1B).

KILLING MOBS (XP each): Cow 20, Pig 20, Sheep 20, Deer 20, Horse 25, Wolf 30, Wildcat 30,
Stag 30, Draugr Skeleton 30, Gorilla 40, Bear 40, Draugr Zombie 40, Cave Golem 50,
Draugr Knight 75. (Magma Golem see SEC 5.)
MINING ORES (XP each): Coal 5, Iron 15, Gold 30, Diamond 40, Moonstone 50.
SMELTING: Iron 10, Gold 15.
CONSUMABLES (XP): Aura XP Fragment 1 level (~0.2s eat), Aura XP Orb 3 levels (1s),
Aura XP Potion 10 levels (1.2s), Splash Aura XP Potion 10 (instant), Aura XP Potion II
15 levels (1.2s), Splash II 15 (instant), Aura XP Arrow 1 level.
(To max out 300 XP ≈ 30 Aura XP Potions. NOTE: the mob "consuming items" table on one wiki
page lists higher round numbers [Orb 300, Potion 1000] — the per-item consumable table
above [Orb=3, Potion=10] is the more consistent source; treat the big numbers as legacy.)
SOURCES OF XP DROPS: killing mobs/players, harvesting crops, mining ores, smelting raw
ores, breaking mob spawners — small chance to drop Aura XP Orbs. Fragments come from
SALVAGING minerals (coal/gold/diamond/moonstone) at a Salvaging Table; 3 Fragments → 1 Orb.

FLOWERS & THE FULL DYE CHART
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Flowers grow in all biomes EXCEPT deserts (Bluebell = Bluebell Forest only; Shadow Rose =
Frozen Badlands only). No hitbox (walk through them). Farm by planting on Tilled/Fertilised
Soil — they spread to adjacent tilled soil over time. Also from Greenville's flower pack.
FLOWER LIST: Poppy, Red Tulip, Orange Tulip, Pink Tulip, White Tulip, Dandelion, Daisy,
Bluebell, Forget-me-not, Allium, Shadow Rose (formerly "Wither Rose"). UNUSED/illegal:
Lily of the Valley, Azure Bluet.

DYEING (done at the ARTISAN BENCH; dyes Planks, Wool, Concrete, Glass/Patterned Glass,
Baked Clay, Ceramic, Beds/Strongbeds, Wood Armor + colored variants, Chalk/Chalk Bricks,
Firecrackers). Note: Cactus can replace Grass, and Poppy ↔ Red Tulip are interchangeable.
FULL COLOR RECIPES:
- Red: Poppy OR Red Tulip
- Orange: Orange Tulip
- Yellow: Dandelion
- Lime: Grass/Cactus + White Tulip
- Green: Grass/Cactus
- Blue: Forget-me-not
- Light Blue: Bluebell
- Cyan: Grass/Cactus + Forget-me-not
- Purple: Poppy + Forget-me-not
- Magenta: Allium
- Pink: Pink Tulip

- Light Gray: White Tulip + Daisy
- Gray: Daisy (or Lily of the Valley)

---------------------------------------------------------------------
PROTECTORS & TRIBES (chunk protection)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
PROTECTOR — protects an entire CHUNK (32×32×32; actually 32×96×32 vertically) it's placed
in, regardless of position within the chunk. Others can't edit protected chunks except
utility blocks (workbench/artisan bench), Moonstone Chests, doors, and trapdoors.
- Craft (Survival/Peaceful, Workbench): 2 Diamonds + 1 Stone.
- Creative: earned by placing/breaking blocks (1st=200, 2nd=600, 3rd=1000, 4th=1800,
  5th=2600, 6th=4200, 7th=5800, 8th=9000 blocks).
- Plots/Doodle Cube: unlimited (area always protected).
- You choose which players may edit your protected chunk. Protectors vanish if the owner
  is offline 30+ days (rumored removed). `/protectors` command lists your protectors' coords.
- Can't place within 192 blocks of true spawn (0,0) — see SEC 4.

TRIBE PROTECTOR — like a Protector but ALL tribe members can edit. Only a tribe LEADER can
make them: 2 Moonstone + 1 Stone (any) in the "manage tribe" menu (b-menu). Disband the
tribe → its tribe protectors vanish.
FACTION PROTECTOR — Factions only; all faction members can use. Not craftable — bought for
100 Gold Coins (faction owner only). Max count scales with faction Power; low power = raidable
(enemies press F in the chunk to break one). This is the only way to break another faction's protector.
TRIBES — player groups; the leader creates the tribe and can make Tribe Protectors. Managed
via the b-menu "manage tribe" screen.

PET FRIENDSHIP SYSTEM (added Nov 10, 2025)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Tame a mob by clicking it while holding food (each food = small tame chance; bring several).
Friendship has 5 levels (hearts); higher = more perks + a Painting at level 4.
TAMING FOODS: Wolf = Bone | Wildcat = any raw/cooked meat (not rotten) | Pig/Cow/Sheep =
Apple | Horse = Apple/Wheat (or ride w/ Saddle) | Deer = Cherry.
PET BASE STATS (HP / walk speed / damage): Pig 300/3.87, Cow 400, Sheep 240, Horse 440/5,
Deer 200, Wolf 320/5.46/10 dmg, Wildcat 315/5.92/10 (w/ Weakness).
LEVEL PERKS (examples): L2 = pet drops its special food item when fed; L3 = per-pet perks;
L4 = a Painting; L5 = advanced perks. Feed after being away → +60 friendship points bonus;
don't feed for 68h+ → lose points (starving).
SPECIAL PET FOOD ITEMS (fed pet drops → craft → effect, all 15s unless noted):
- Wildcat → Caught Fish → Fish Fillet → "Pickpocketing" (steal items on attack).
- Wolf → Meaty Bone → Blindness Pebbles (throw → Blindness on target).
- Horse → Oats → Porridge → "Wall Climbing" (climb any block like a ladder).
- Cow → Cow's Milk → Cheese → "Air Walk" 15s (walk on air at last block's height; jumping cancels).
- Pig → Truffle → Truffle Oil → "Bounciness" (bounce on any block).
- Sheep → Sheep's Milk → Yoghurt Pot → "Lifesteal" (steal HP on hit).
- Deer → Acorn → Acorn Jelly → 2 min "Haste" (faster block breaking).
PAINTINGS (7, one per tameable pet, at L4): Grant Wool (Sheep), Stampede (Cow), Unforgotten
Pig (Pig), Sunbathed Gallope (Horse), Dreaming Canine (Wolf), A Doe through the Green (Deer),
Whiskers (Wildcat).
Pets can be renamed with a Nametag at friendship L1. Poop (from pets) → Fertiliser.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
BEDS — RESPAWN POINTS & SLEEPING PERKS
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Beds are utility blocks (mine with Axe). Two core functions:
- SET SPAWN POINT: tap a bed to set your respawn there. You can only have ONE spawn point
  at a time (on a bed OR a Checkpoint Block) — dying teleports you to the LAST one you set.
- RESTED PERK: sleep (stay) on a bed 10+ seconds to pick ONE rested effect: increased
  Damage, Haste (mining speed), Speed, Farming Yield, or extra Aura XP gained. Only one
  rested effect at a time; picking another overwrites it.
GOTCHAS: spikes on top of a bed damage you while sleeping (die → respawn where you clicked
from). Fully enclosing a bed = you respawn glitched inside the blocks (must break out).
RECIPES (see SEC 7): 3 Wool (any color) + 3 Wood Planks. White & Red beds craft at Workbench
OR Artisan Bench; all other 14 colors = Artisan Bench only. Leaf Bed = 2 Leaves + 2 Planks
(Workbench/Artisan/handcraft). 16 colors + Leaf variant.
PER-MODE: Survival/Peaceful/Creative/Worlds/Custom = spawn + perks + homes. BEDWARS = your
team's respawn anchor; destroy enemy beds to stop their respawns (can't sleep in Bedwars).
Lucky Towers = given randomly, used to build up (can't set spawn). Pirates = decoration
only. Greenville = restores energy (buy from shop; can't craft). PARKOUR use: beds are 2
blocks long → "block clutching" across 6–7 block gaps; sleeping mid-fall cancels ALL fall damage.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
DOORS, TRAPDOORS & LADDERS
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- DOORS: block + item; tap to open/close. Closed by default. The door itself has no hitbox,
  but a closed door has a 2-block-tall hitbox behind it; open doors have no hitbox (walk
  through). Craft: 2 Wood Planks of a type → 1 Door of that wood (all 11+ tree types:
  Maple/Aspen/Pine/Cedar/Jungle/Palm/Plum/Pear/Cherry/Spectral/Mango). Workbench.
- TRAPDOORS: like doors, closed by default, tap to toggle. Closed trapdoor = 1-block hitbox
  below it (opening removes it). Can also be CLIMBED like a ladder (but slower than a ladder).
  Craft: Wood Planks at Workbench.
- LADDERS (incl. Iron Ladder): climbable. Note the "ladder launching" parkour tech. Doors,
  trapdoors, and beds are all usable even inside another player's protected chunk (SEC 8).

COCONUT (thrown projectile — the uncracked form)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- Coconut (uncracked): a THROWABLE. On landing it becomes a Cracked Coconut. If it hits a
  PLAYER: gives them 8 seconds of Speed BUT deals 5 damage. From Fruity Palm Leaves (Jungle).
- Cracked Coconut (food, SEC 5): eat for 8s Speed. Drinking/using it can make a Splash Speed
  Potion for PvP escapes (warning: the attacker caught in the splash also gets Speed — a
  drunk Speed Potion is safer). Coconut + Water Bottle → Speed Potion. 4 Coconut → Coconut Block.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
PARKOUR & MOVEMENT TECH (real in-game mechanics)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- Base jump = up to 5 blocks. With Bunny Hopping: 6-block jump (7 with double/triple b-hop).
- BUNNY HOP: sprint-jump the instant you land. Chaining gives more speed; triple b-hop = max
  (quadruple+ = same as triple). Toggle "Disable Bunny Hopping" in Settings (SEC 4).
- NEO JUMPS: jump around a pillar/wall — 4-neo normally, up to 6.5 with b-hop.
- ICE: walking on ice boosts speed (+1.4) → 9-block ice jumps with b-hop.
- WATER JUMPS: first contact with water lets you jump on top without sinking; 5-block
  vertical water jump by placing water on a wall.
- CHECKPOINTS: Bed / Checkpoint Block (spawn point), Homes (/sethome, /home, or B-menu, if
  not disabled), Yellow Wool (Greenville), Block of Gold (Bloxd Hop).
- LAUNCHING: Knockback/Splash potions, Fireballs, and Moonstone Explosives can launch you
  for parkour/escapes.
